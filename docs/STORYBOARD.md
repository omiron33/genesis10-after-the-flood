# Storyboard: After the Flood (Genesis 10)

Ultra tier, 1080p60, every frame drawn in code. One journey carries the song: down from the ark on Ararat over the new-dry earth, through the lands of each of Noah's three sons (Japheth's green coasts and islands, Ham's hot river country with Nimrod's cities, Mizraim's cliffs and Canaan's hills and border, Shem's stair and plateau in the east), and back to Ararat at night with the fires of the nations spread across the earth below. The light runs dawn to night through Japheth and Ham, a new morning in Canaan to a Salt Sea sunset, dusk to dawn in the east, and night on Ararat at the end. No people are shown; the nations are their names in their lands. Every word is part of the world, in the form that fits each place: stone, bronze, driftwood, salt, glazed brick and gold standing as 3D letters; names painted on hulls, sails and terrace walls; cut into mud, sand, cliffs, stair risers and rock.
